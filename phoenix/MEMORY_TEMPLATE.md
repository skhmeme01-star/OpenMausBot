## Maintenance rules

- Store durable, useful information only; omit transient details and transcripts.
- Date every entry and identify its source or confirmation. Label uncertainty.
- Search and read existing entries before relying on history or adding duplicates.
- Correct or replace superseded claims; do not leave conflicting claims as current.
- Keep only the minimum personal detail needed. Never store passwords, tokens,
  secret keys, or unnecessary third-party information.
- Preserve unrelated entries during edits. Verify a save before claiming it persisted.
- Periodically remove stale material and consolidate duplicates. If nothing durable
  was learned, make no change. Ask the owner when a material conflict cannot be resolved.
